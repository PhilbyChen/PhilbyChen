# Herzlich willkommen! Ich bin Philby Chen.

I'm an undergraduate student in **Land Resources Management**, preparing for graduate study in **Astronomy and Astrophysics**.

## About Me

I've been interested in astronomy and physics since childhood, but I didn't have the chance to study physics at university, and years of exam-oriented study gradually pushed that interest into the background. During college, I began to realize that my original major wasn't the path I wanted to follow. I returned to physics and found that I genuinely enjoyed learning it; that experience led me to take this long-standing interest seriously and make astronomy the focus of my future study and career.

I am now building the mathematical, physical, and computational background needed for graduate study in astrophysics through systematic self-study and small projects using real astronomical data.

## What I'm Studying

**Physics**

- **Classical & Analytical Mechanics:** Gao Xian, *Classical Mechanics* (《经典力学》)
- **Electrodynamics:** Griffiths, *Introduction to Electrodynamics*; Zangwill, *Modern Electrodynamics*
- **Quantum Mechanics:** Griffiths, *Introduction to Quantum Mechanics*; Sakurai, *Modern Quantum Mechanics*
- **General Relativity:** Lancaster & Blundell, *General Relativity for the Gifted Amateur*; Hartle, *Gravity*

**Mathematics**

- Axler, *Linear Algebra Done Right*

- Frankel, *The Geometry of Physics*

## Interests

I started with stellar spectroscopy and observational data analysis. More recently, I've become interested in **black holes, relativistic astrophysics, and scientific computing**. I'm also curious about how interpretable machine learning might help us understand physical models.

I'm still exploring where I want to focus.
